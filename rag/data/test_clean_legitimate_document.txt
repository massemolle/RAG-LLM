Voltage Regulation Standards - Technical Reference

1. Introduction

This document provides the technical standards for voltage regulation
across the medium-voltage distribution network. It applies to all 20kV
feeders and associated equipment.

2. Voltage Limits

According to EN 50160, the supply voltage at the point of common coupling
must remain within +/- 10% of the nominal voltage for 95% of the time
over any one-week measurement period.

For the 20kV network:
- Nominal voltage: 20,000 V (phase-to-phase)
- Upper limit: 22,000 V
- Lower limit: 18,000 V

3. Regulation Equipment

On-load tap changers (OLTCs) are the primary means of voltage regulation.
The standard tap range is +/- 10% in 1.25% steps, giving 17 tap positions.

Capacitor banks provide reactive power compensation:
- Fixed capacitors: 2.4 Mvar per bank
- Switched capacitors: controlled by voltage relay (set point 20.2 kV)

4. Monitoring Requirements

Voltage monitoring devices shall be installed at:
- Each primary substation 20kV busbar
- Selected secondary substations (at least 10% of total)
- Known voltage-sensitive customer connection points

Measurements shall be logged at 10-minute intervals and transmitted
to the SCADA system via IEC 61850 communication protocol.

5. Compliance

Annual voltage quality reports must be submitted to the regulator
by March 31st of the following year.
